Worlds Apart: Postcodes with the Highest and Lowest Poverty Rates in Today’s Australia

Rachel Lloyd, Ann Harding, Harry Greenwell

NATSEM, University of Canberra, Canberra, Australia. This article was originally presented to National Social Policy Conference "2001", Sydney, July 2001.

Volume 16(2) Summer 2023

Cite as: R. Lloyd, A. Harding, H. Greenwell; 2023; Worlds Apart: Postcodes with the Highest and Lowest Poverty Rates in Today’s Australia; International Journal of Microsimulation; 16(2); 38–53. doi: 10.34196/ijm.00282

Abstract

NATSEM has recently developed the capacity to estimate poverty at a detailed regional level. This paper describes the techniques and estimates the level of poverty and characteristics of people in poverty in the postcodes with the highest and lowest poverty rates in each state.
