Modelling the cost of ill health in Health&WealthMOD (Version II): Lost labour force participation, income and taxation, and the impact of disease prevention

Deborah Schofield, Rupendra Shrestha, Emily Callander, Richard Pervical, Simon Kelly, Megan Passey, Susan Fletcher

NHMRC Clinical Trials Centre and School of Public Health, Faculty of Medicine, University of Sydney, Australia; The National Centre for Social and Economic Modelling, University of Canberra, Australia; Department of Rural Health, School of Public Health, Faculty of Medicine, University of Sydney, Northern Rivers University, Australia

Volume 4(3) Winter 2011

Cite as: D. Schofield, R. Shrestha, E. Callander, R. Pervical, S. Kelly, M. Passey, S. Fletcher; 2011; Modelling the cost of ill health in Health&WealthMOD (Version II): Lost labour force participation, income and taxation, and the impact of disease prevention; International Journal of Microsimulation; 4(3); 32–36. doi: 10.34196/ijm.00058

Abstract

This paper provides a detailed description of the construction of Health&WealthMOD (Version II). It is Australia’s only microsimulation model of health and illness and their impacts on labour force participation, income, wealth and government revenue and expenditure. In this paper, we describe Health&WealthMOD (Version II) and its architecture, the application of the model, and some of the results it has produced.
