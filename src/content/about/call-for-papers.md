## "EUROMOD turns 30"

**Call for Papers**

Guest Editor: Chrysa Leventi (JRC, European Commission)

**Submission deadline:** 31 December 2026

**Background**

[EUROMOD](https://euromod-web.jrc.ec.europa.eu/), the tax-benefit microsimulation model for the European Union, turned 30 in 2026. The anniversary was marked by the EUROMOD Annual Meeting and Research Workshop, held in Brussels from 23 to 25 September 2026 and organised by the EUROMOD team at the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (JRC). The workshop presented the role of the model in evidence-based policymaking and showcased the variety of its applications.

Building on this momentum, the International Journal of Microsimulation invites contributions for a Special Issue on research that uses, extends or reflects on EUROMOD. The call is open to authors who presented at the workshop and to all other researchers working with EUROMOD.

**Who can submit**

- Researchers who presented a paper at the EUROMOD Research Workshop 2026 are warmly invited to submit a full version of their work.
- Researchers who did not take part in the workshop are equally welcome, provided the paper is related to EUROMOD.

All submissions go through the same peer-review process, and presenting at the workshop does not guarantee acceptance.

**Submission guidelines**

- **Article types.** Research articles. Word limits follow the <a href="/about/submission-guidelines">IJM author guidelines</a>.
- **Originality.** Papers must be original and not under consideration elsewhere.
- **Transparency.** Authors should state the EUROMOD version, input data and policy year(s) used, and are encouraged to share code, parameter changes and replication material where data access rules allow.
- **Format and portal.** Please follow the <a href="/about/submission-guidelines">IJM author guidelines</a> and submit through the journal's <a href="https://www.epress.ac.uk/ijm/webforms/author.php">online submission system</a>. Select the special issue "EUROMOD turns 30" when submitting.

**Review process**

All submissions are peer reviewed by at least two independent referees in line with IJM's standard <a href="/about/editorial-policy">editorial policy</a>. The Guest Editor may desk-reject papers outside the scope of the call.
